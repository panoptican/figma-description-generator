# Focus thumbnail variants

Three editable Figma explorations based on the approved single-component Focus design. All exports are 1920 × 1080 PNGs. The title, captions, descriptions, and handwritten notes are live text; the illustrations are editable vector shapes.

- [Bulk generation](https://www.figma.com/design/BMqc0z8FUhrcWrjM2G5wkj?node-id=29-2) — `bulk-generation.png`. Six selected components feed six descriptions. Caption: “Your whole library. Described.”
- [Pencil drafts](https://www.figma.com/design/BMqc0z8FUhrcWrjM2G5wkj?node-id=29-7) — `pencil-drafts.png`. Three illustrative drafts connected by dotted pencil lines. Caption: “Good descriptions start here.”
- [Editor’s redline](https://www.figma.com/design/BMqc0z8FUhrcWrjM2G5wkj?node-id=29-12) — `editors-redline.png`. Red-ink corrections and an editorial note lead to a clearer description. Caption: “Less vague. More useful.”

Typography: Inter throughout, with Caveat handwriting and IBM Plex Mono draft labels in the paper directions. Reviewed in Figma at full composition and 480-pixel thumbnail scale; all fonts resolved and no raster fills are present.
